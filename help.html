🙏 Pray

The Pray tab is the point of the app. It walks you through your prayer list one category at a time, in the order you've set. If you've turned on a devotional, that comes first — read it and tap Continue.

What each category screen shows

For the category you're on, PrayIt shows two things:

  • Scheduled requests that are due today — grouped by person, and sorted with the most frequent (Daily) first.
  • A page from the rotation — a handful of your unscheduled requests for this category, under an "Also praying for" heading. How many appear at once is the category's reveal size (set in Admin).

If there's nothing due and nothing in rotation for a category, it simply says so. Tap Continue to move to the next category, or Finish on the last one.

Marking a request

Tap the green circle next to a request to mark it Prayed. It drops out of view for the rest of the day. If it was a rotation item, the next one takes its place — so you can keep going as long as you like, and wherever you stop is where you pick up next time.

Swipe actions

✎ Update ✓ Answered

Swipe right on a request to add an Update — a dated note on how it's going, without closing the request. Swipe left to mark it Answered with a note; it moves to the Answered list and stops appearing in the flow.

Show More

If a category has more unscheduled requests than fit on today's page, a Show More button reveals another batch of the same size. It's a peek ahead — marking something Prayed resets it.

Start Over

After the last category you'll see an All Done screen with a Start Over button. It just lets you walk the categories again from the top — it does not un-pray anything or rewind your place in the rotation.

👥 People

The People tab is your roster — everyone and everything you pray for. Each row shows a name and how many active requests it has. Tap a row to open that person or event.

Two list styles

In Admin you can choose how this list looks:

  • Grouped by category — sections for Family, Church, and so on.
  • Flat A–Z — one alphabetical list, sorted by last name, with an A–Z scrubber down the side to jump around quickly.

Adding someone

Tap +, enter a name, and pick a category (the default category is pre-selected). Turn on "This is an event, not a person" for things like Elders & Church or Women's ministry. You can type their first prayer request right on the same screen, or leave it blank and add requests later.

A person's detail screen

Shows their category, any notes, their Active Requests, and an Answered section once something's been closed out. From the ⋯ menu you can add a request or edit the person. Swipe a request to delete it, or tap the seal icon to mark it answered.

Search

The search bar matches both names and the text of requests — so searching "salvation" finds every person with a request about it.

📋 All Prayers

A single combined view of every request, across all people and categories. Use the toggle at the top to switch between:

  • Active — everything still being prayed for
  • Answered — everything closed out, with its answer note
  • All — both together

Requests stay grouped under their category and person, so it reads like a printed prayer list.

Sharing / exporting a list

Tap the share button in the top corner to export the current view as plain text — ready to paste into an email, a message, or a notes app, or to hand to someone else who prays with you.

⚙️ Admin

Everything you configure lives here.

Categories

Tap Edit, then drag to reorder — this is the order you pray through them. Tap a category to rename it, set how many requests it reveals at a time in the Pray flow, or make it the default for new people (only one category can be the default).

People List

The Group by category toggle switches the People tab between sections and the flat A–Z list.

Daily Devotional

Pick a source — None, My Own, Spurgeon's Morning and Evening, or Streams in the Desert. With My Own selected, a Manage My Devotionals link appears. See Devotionals below.

Prayer Schedule

Balance scheduled prayers spreads your fixed-schedule requests evenly across the calendar. It's on by default. Balance Now re-runs it on demand. See Schedule balancing below.

Backup & Restore

Export a JSON backup, or import one. Import always merges — see Backup & Restore.

Your Data

A quick count of categories, people/events, active requests, and answered prayers.

Delete All Data

Permanently removes every category, person, and request. There's a confirmation step — export a backup first.

🔁 Rotation

A request with no fixed schedule goes into its category's rotation pool. You don't see the whole pool every day — you see a page of it, oldest first, and the app remembers how far you've actually prayed.

How the page moves

Each day's page starts right after the last request you prayed through. Mark that page done and the next page begins where it left off — today, tomorrow, or next week. It doesn't matter how many you get through in one sitting.

Why it doesn't skip anyone: if one person has two requests and you happen to pray the second before the first, the rotation won't jump past the first. It only moves forward once a run of requests is genuinely done.

Set the page size per category

In Admin, each category has a "Reveal at least N at a time" setting. A category with a lot of people might reveal 5; a short one might reveal 2. Show More in the Pray flow reveals another batch when you want to go further.

📅 Schedule balancing

When you give a request a fixed schedule — Daily, Weekly, every few days — it comes due on its own rhythm. The catch: if you add five "every 5 days" requests in one sitting, they'd all come due together every fifth day, leaving the days between empty.

What balancing does

Balance scheduled prayers (Admin → Prayer Schedule) staggers your fixed-schedule requests so that roughly the same number come due each day — across your whole list, whatever the mix of intervals. It never touches rotation requests or anything you've marked answered.

  • On by default. While it's on, each new scheduled request is placed on the lightest day as you create it.
  • Balance Now re-spreads everything on demand — run it whenever a day starts feeling heavy.
  • Turning the toggle off then back on also re-balances everything.
Note: balancing shifts when a request next comes due, not its interval. A "Weekly" request stays weekly — it just may move to a different day of the week. It also drifts over time as you pray things at odd hours, so re-running it occasionally is normal.

📖 Devotionals

When a source is set, the Pray tab opens with a short devotional — a verse and a prayer or reflection — before the categories. Choose the source in Admin → Daily Devotional.

My Own

Write your own entries in Manage My Devotionals. Each has a reference, the verse text, and a prayer. They're shown one per day, in the order you arrange them, looping back to the start after the last one. The cycle begins at entry #1 on the day you add your first one.

Bundled books

Spurgeon's Morning and Evening and Streams in the Desert are included in full (both public domain). They're keyed to the calendar, so you get the entry for today's date.

Importing your own from a spreadsheet

In Manage My Devotionals, tap Import from CSV. Build the list in any spreadsheet app and export it as a .csv with these three columns, in this order, with a header row on top:

ColumnWhat goes in itExample
ReferenceThe verse referencePsalm 23:1
VerseThe verse textThe Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.
PrayerYour prayer or reflectionFather, thank you that you provide…

Imported entries are added after any you already have. A row whose reference and verse already exist is skipped, so re-importing an updated sheet only adds what's new.

If an import reports "0 imported": it usually means the rows were all duplicates, or the file has unusual characters — most often curly "smart quotes" that got scrambled when the sheet was saved as CSV. Retype those as plain straight quotes and export again.

💾 Backup & Restore

Your data syncs through iCloud automatically, but a local backup is still worth keeping.

Finding the tabs on iPad: the tab bar runs across the top. If a tab isn't visible, tap the > chevron at the right end to reveal the rest.

Export

Admin → Export Backup creates a JSON file and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files, Dropbox, or email it to yourself. It contains every category, person, request, update, and custom devotional.

Import

Admin → Import Backup, then pick a JSON file. Import merges — it adds what's in the file and never deletes or overwrites what's already there. If the backup has a default category, your current default is un-flagged so there's still only one.

Tip: if you just used Delete All Data and iCloud sync is on, wait 30–60 seconds before importing. iCloud may still be processing the deletions, and importing too soon can cause a conflict.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between "Prayed" and "Answered"?

Prayed (the green circle) just means you prayed for it today — it hides for the rest of the day and comes back tomorrow on its schedule or in rotation. Answered closes the request out for good, with a note about how it was answered, and moves it to the Answered list.

I'm praying about something ongoing that will never really be "answered." What do I do?

Keep it active and use Update (swipe right in the Pray flow, or the History section on the request). Updates are dated notes on how something's going — they don't change the schedule, the rotation, or whether it's answered.

Why did a scheduled request move to a different day?

That's schedule balancing (Admin → Prayer Schedule), which is on by default. It spreads your fixed-schedule requests so no single day is overloaded. The interval is unchanged — a Weekly request is still weekly, it just may fall on a different day. Turn the toggle off if you'd rather schedules stay exactly as entered.

Should I give a request a schedule or leave it unscheduled?

Use a schedule for things you want to pray on a set rhythm — a spouse daily, a missionary weekly. Leave it unscheduled for the long tail of people and requests you want to get to regularly but not on a fixed day; those rotate a page at a time so you cover everyone over the course of a week or two.

Does PrayIt work without iCloud?

Yes. All your data is stored on the device whether or not iCloud is on — you just won't get sync to your other devices. If you're not using iCloud, export a backup now and then so you have a copy.

Can I use it on my iPad or Mac?

PrayIt is built for iPhone and runs on iPad. iCloud keeps your list in sync when you move between devices.

Does "Start Over" undo my prayers for the day?

No. It only sends you back to the first category so you can walk through again. Anything you marked Prayed stays that way, and your place in the rotation keeps moving forward.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Head to the Support page and send an email. Include your build number (Admin → About) and what you expected versus what happened.

What's inside

A calmer way to keep a prayer list

🙏

The daily flow

Open the Pray tab and move through your categories in order. Each screen shows what's due today — mark a request prayed, jot an update, or close it out as answered.

👥

People & events

Keep a roster of everyone and everything you pray for, each with its own list of requests. Browse grouped by category, or as one flat A–Z list with a scrubber.

🔁

Rotation & schedules

Give a request a fixed rhythm — daily, weekly, every few days — or leave it unscheduled and let it rotate. Either way you cover everyone over time without a wall of text each morning.

📖

Daily devotional

Start each session with a verse and a prayer. Write your own to cycle through, or use a bundled public-domain classic — Spurgeon's Morning and Evening or Streams in the Desert.

How it works

Set it up once, pray from it every day

1

Add your people and events

Sort them into categories — Family, Church, Friends, Praise. One category can be the default for anything new.

2

Attach prayer requests

Add as many requests as you like under each person. Give a request a schedule, or leave it open to come up in rotation.

3

Open the Pray tab

Walk through one category at a time. Mark things prayed as you go — tomorrow picks up right where you left off.

🙏

Your list, always with you

PrayIt syncs across your iPhone and iPad via iCloud. No account to create — just the Apple ID you already use. Nothing is ever sent to anyone else.
